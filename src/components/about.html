About Us

Harborshine

Cleaning Company!

Nathalie Jara is the founder and owner of Harborshine Cleaning Company. After several years working as an independent cleaner, she saw the opportunity to create a more refined, detail-oriented approach to professional cleaning for her clients, prioritizing quality over quantity.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, Nathalie launched Harborshine to deliver meticulous care for residential homes, vacation rentals, and commercial offices across San Diego and the surrounding areas. She is equally dedicated to building a more equitable workplace, guided by a simple yet powerful belief:
“No judgment.”

So please give us the opportunity to provide the Harborshine experience for you so you can experience our company motto:
All things shining!
